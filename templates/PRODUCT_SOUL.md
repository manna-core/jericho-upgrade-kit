## What it must never become

- A system that pretends stale data is fresh.
- A system that exposes company or customer data casually.
- A system that makes users trust AI output without context.
- A system that becomes harder to sell because the builder context is hidden in chat.

## Product taste rules

- Prefer useful workflows over decorative surfaces.
- Prefer truthful status over impressive-looking dashboards.
- Prefer small verified upgrades over large speculative rewrites.
- Preserve the selling motion and demo value.
